# What must be decided

Consider two executions with identical local observations up to acceptance. In one, the controller authorizes Bob on a source-chain branch. In the other an independently valid branch authorizes Carol. Delay all cross-partition messages until after both recipients decide. A verifier accepting either unrestricted choice in isolation accepts both in the combined execution. Deterministic tie-breaking after delivery gives eventual convergence at the cost of reversing a previous acceptance. This argument applies to services promising irrevocability under arbitrary delay and unrestricted choice. Preassigning recipients changes the premise.

For a single round of durable reservations, two q-subsets of n overlap in at least 2q−n. An overlap larger than the lifetime equivocation budget e forces one consistent signer to see both would-be decisions. With up to s silent identities and no other contention, progress needs q≤n−s, hence n≥e+2s+1. At 7 witnesses and 5 votes, overlap is 3. At 8 and 6 it is 4. Restoring three identities over the lifetime invalidates the first policy even if they were never faulty simultaneously. Atomic persistence must precede releasing a signature.

Let k distinct authenticated permanent closure reports contain a(x) reservations for candidate x. Unreported witnesses, matching reports and at most e inconsistent negative reports bound possible signatures by min(n,n−k+a(x)+e). A hidden *or future* certificate is excluded exactly when k−max a(x)>n−q+e in this information model. Include an unmentioned candidate with count zero. When the inequality fails, some admissible candidate may still lack any real certificate. Promoting it creates a new positive decision without authority. Refund identity derives solely from the pinned parent and domain. No packet delivery deadline enters the proof.

## Composition theorem

Assume authenticated unique genesis, collision-resistant domain-separated commitments, per-input quorum intersection, honest signers enforcing the same transition predicate, disjoint initial units, and a terminal oracle that answers at most one truthfully funded outcome per sale forever. Induct on verification DAG depth. Conflicting certified successors of one parent contradict the overlap lemma. Conflicting deterministic exercises of one certificate require incompatible evidence from the pinned oracle. Distinct authenticated parents have disjoint footprints by the predecessor invariant. Multi-input children become usable only with the complete joint certificate; one parent reservation cannot activate a partial child. The induction establishes safety even under arbitrary withholding. It says nothing about how many branches finish. Credentials of a rightful controller allow an adversary to choose any transition that controller could authorize.

The terminal oracle is an extra assumption about the payment system. Equating it with RAVE execution would invalidate the theorem. Permanent closing reports only cover the decisions their signers actually fenced. Payment-issuer rollback cannot be repaired with NFT closure reports.

## Relation to prior work

Quorum intersection, blocking sets, linear resources, escrow allocation, optimistic concurrency read/write sets, Merkle membership and blocking distributed commit are established mechanisms. Gray and Lamport's transaction commit analysis explains why preparation alone can block under coordinator failure. Herlihy's atomic swaps give a useful comparison when both ledgers implement the required contracts and timing assumptions. Phase 2 already establishes handoff, compact batches and predetermined grants. Phase 3 contributes the concrete exercise/install boundary, executable restricted guard inference and an end-to-end byte profile with measured costs. The results establish no new consensus lower bound or general solution to fair exchange.
