# Outstanding boundaries

| Issue | Impact | Evidence required to close it |
|---|---|---|
| Native payment terminality, collectible funds and no competing reclaim | A valid commercial execution can be followed by a different economic outcome. | Authoritative source-domain verifier and complete failure model. Public unyt and unyt-app Cargo.toml lookups returned 404 through the connector. |
| Availability after irreversible decisions | Entitlement can vest without either buyer or a later verifier obtaining the bytes. | Independent durable replicas, authenticated retrieval and tested failure thresholds. |
| Whole-image rollback of votes, shares, issuer database or old epoch keys | Hidden signatures outlive reset local memory. | External monotonic anchor or explicit lifetime compromise accounting. |
| Genesis and cross-application redemption | Two apps may create independent wrapped claims, or legacy ownership may remain usable. | Unique export/import decision and acceptance change for all authoritative clients. |
| Issuer controls the simulated credit and can withhold service | Seller has no independently redeemable Unyt value. Anyone can void an unresolved funded sale in this profile. | Decide custody, payment confirmation and cancellation authority explicitly. |
| Policy evolution and freshness | Existing envelopes certify historical single-generation outcomes. Copying an old proof cannot authorize a new current-owner assertion. | Lineage-aware successor, epoch transition, canonical vectors and explicit live reservation protocol. |
| Hostile operational environment | Loopback workers, generated files and SQLite do not demonstrate multi-operator security. Host inputs can cost more than the inner verifier cap. | Deployment isolation, key admission, availability budgets and independent audits. |
| Partial validation coverage | No WAN, full hNFT/DEX Sweettest matrix, private Unyt production validator, networked FROST, general agreement compiler or external proof availability implementation. | Run compatible isolated workspaces and measure with representative validators and network topology. |
