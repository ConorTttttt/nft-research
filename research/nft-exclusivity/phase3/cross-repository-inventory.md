The organization survey also found unyt-sandbox-iroh, unytctl, joining-service, watchtower and wind-tunnel-unyt. Their presence is insufficient evidence of a terminal payment API. This inventory covers selected implementation paths and applicable branches. Only the suites listed in test-results.md are independently reproduced.

Holochain-Time's unyt/verified_time_release/execution_code.rhai fixes acceptable oracle keys and DNAs, bounds the number of examined statements, and uses the (f+1)-th largest lower bound when at most f distinct oracles can lie. A past true lower bound remains true for a monotonic release schedule. Expiring a previously vested right needs different semantics. This template is a candidate for precommitted time tranches.
