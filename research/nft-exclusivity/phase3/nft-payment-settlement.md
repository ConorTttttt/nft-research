# One terminal choice shared by title and payment

1. Alice controls a test genesis slot. Its policy fixes Bob, seller, price 10, unit 0, agreement byte hash, network and issuer. The listing is Alice's signed proposal. The adapter allocates one dedicated funded test coupon per slot. This restricted genesis makes duplicate imports and flexible listings separate research problems.
2. Five of seven native workers durably reserve the parent and sign the same flat Phase 2 statement. Under this domain the child is a conditional grant. The witness installation path rejects an ordinary Phase 2 installation from genesis. Clients must know this acceptance policy.
3. Verify the grant before reserving Bob's coupon. Bob signs the exact sale digest under the reserve domain. The ledger locks its row in SQLite using BEGIN IMMEDIATE. A submitted authorization for another sale cannot reserve this one.
4. Pay and void compete on that row. Pay requires a reservation, runs the pinned hNFT script using rave_engine 0.15.0 and checks that its one allocation names the exact seller, source and unit-map amount. The row's terminal paid state is the seller's simulated credit. Void consumes the sale-specific coupon into the refund outcome, including before reservation. No general account balances or withdrawal API are implemented. Any submitter may request void. This permits cancellation griefing.
5. The same commit stores the grant and final allocation before signing terminal evidence. A crash after commit can reproduce the signature from the row. Repeated pay, void and materialization are idempotent for the same outcome. The other outcome is rejected. The issuer must never roll back, clone its signing authority or certify a state outside this machine.
6. Anyone with the grant and terminal evidence derives the same child and controller. Its entitlement vests when the ledger settles. Installing it at witnesses enables an ordinary onward CEL sale under the same roster. Both the paid buyer and voided seller paths exercise this right in the demo. A fresh choice still needs its own quorum.
7. A stock Holochain conductor publishes and retrieves the paid composite proof. Its DNA properties pin the domain. The absent or unavailable proof path leaves the client pending. Reconciliation fetches the exact archived grant and terminal proof from the surviving ledger.

## Economic guarantee

Under an honest durable ledger, paid implies a fixed, recoverable Bob entitlement and excludes a refund. Void implies Alice retains a successor and excludes payment. The protocol tolerates a disappearing seller after certification. A disappearing buyer before reservation cannot cause a debit. A third party may void the certified grant if the ledger is reachable. It cannot unlock an unresolved CEL parent without the Phase 2 exclusion condition. The demo does not implement pre-certification genesis reset installation.

Materialized delivery can lag payment without bound. If Bob's requirement is possession of usable evidence or immediate resale at the instant Alice is credited, this protocol fails that definition of fair exchange. If the ledger withholds or permanently loses the only evidence, Bob can remain unable to exercise even though the mathematical entitlement is irrevocable. Replication needs durable, independently retrievable full bodies and a release rule tied to availability. Content hashes alone cannot supply the bytes.

A proof of payment must bind source network and unit authority, unique debit, buyer, seller, amount, sale identity, grant, rules and terminality. Escrow reservation, intent, RAVE output and chain-local receipt each prove a narrower statement. The lab issuer compresses the evidence for one test realm into its own signature. Compromise or rollback of this issuer defeats the exchange even with all NFT witnesses honest.

The mint-enabled fixture first calls real hNFT mint_nft on a disposable stock conductor. It derives a test realm from the returned source DNA and mint action and designates slot zero as the imported test NFT. It uses separate test keys for the payment/ownership workflow. The needed mint-to-seller authority and single-import policy are supplied out of band. The end-to-end result demonstrates the application boundary but does not make legacy hNFT title exclusive across both systems.
