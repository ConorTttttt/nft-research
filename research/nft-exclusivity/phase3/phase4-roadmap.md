# Phase 4 priorities and exit criteria

1. **Native payment evidence.** Agree with the Unyt implementation owners on precise source-DNA, agreement, lane, fee, parked-source, debit, receiver and terminality statements. Obtain validator code or authoritative executable contracts. Test a withheld payment followed by reclaim, executor disappearance, fork, migration carry and duplicate collection. Exit when an independent verifier can accept paid and permanently exclude void on the same funded input. Otherwise retain a labelled escrow issuer or a common ledger.
2. **Evidence availability and recovery.** Persist grant body, funding authorization, terminal record and required dependencies before irreversible acknowledgment. Pin an availability policy and exercise recovery after losing the executor and an archive. Document exactly how many replica failures strand each party. Independently demonstrate an external rollback anchor for signers and issuer, or keep that failure inside the lifetime fault budget.
3. **Unique issuance and portable import.** Bind a real hNFT mint, serial, DNA and immutable collection policy to one exclusive genesis. A trusted registry, restricted native acceptance or certified export must prevent the same bearer asset remaining spendable in two realms. Provide compatibility behavior for old hNFT clients. Demonstrate export, handoff, subsequent transfer and rejected second import with disjoint committees.
4. **Executable rights compiler.** Encode conservation, controller scope, finite interval partitions and read/write predicates in a typed fragment. Emit guard sets and witnesses of necessity. Add lease-preserving and vacant sales on HoloHomes data, bounded interval splitting, delegate/revoke races, and multi-roster prepare/fence or explicit blocking semantics.
5. **Certified checkpoints and operations.** Build a complete-frontier attestation, portable inclusion/consistency vectors and an availability protocol. Exercise witness restart against trusted monotonic state. Benchmark multi-host WAN partitions, handoff availability, concurrent independent assets and bounded WASM costs. Evaluate FROST only after matching the baseline's accountability and recovery duties.
