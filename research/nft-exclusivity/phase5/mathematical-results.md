# Minimum coordination and bilateral preparation

Let an input version be identified by its economic origin, serial/version and permanently pinned admission policy. A certificate chooses exactly one authorized successor of that version. Two actions commute if every ordering preserves validity and yields the same recognized state and entitlements. A **minimal incompatible set** is a jointly inadmissible set whose proper subsets are all admissible. Pairwise conflicts alone are insufficient for fungible capacity: three reservations of 4 against 10 each fit in pairs. A pairwise “no conflict” compiler would authorize 12. The limited resource language admits fixed nonnegative unit amounts, exact parents, conserved partitions, immutable grant terms and one outcome per version. It makes no inference about arbitrary Rhai. [Executable capacity checks](formal-models/capacity.py)

**Capacity bound.** If isolated replicas may each grant the entire same remaining capacity, histories with indistinguishable local prefixes can merge to an overdraw. Any system accepting such independent grants must either restrict the locally available budgets or establish a common decision before unconditional acceptance. Partitioning balance into conserved exclusive allowances once suffices: for disjoint budgets bᵢ with sum at most available funds, any local charges xᵢ ≤ bᵢ have sum at most available funds. Credit additionally requires a non-revocable guarantee for outstanding amounts or a liability-aware limit-change protocol. The checker enumerated 1,001 small partition/consumption combinations. This is escrow arithmetic, an established mechanism. A later application of the partition theorem cannot retroactively fence the original money. [Escrow transactional method](https://doi.org/10.1145/7239.7265)

**Two irrevocable preparations.** Let T and P be fixed, admissible versions of the NFT and capacity. Each independently chooses PREPARE(g), DECLINE(g), or an incompatible disposition. Bind the shared intent g to resources and versions, domains, price, unit, principals, beneficiary, rule version and authority roots. In the restricted sale, each PREPARE is the owner's irrevocable consent to both effects if and only if the other input chooses the same sale. Either side can prepare first. Neither preparation alone transfers beneficial entitlement. Define Paid(g) by the conjunction of the two matching choices. Define Void(g) by an authentic permanent decision on either exact input inconsistent with PREPARE(g). A hidden choice, timeout, missing deposit or administrative declaration is insufficient.

**Safety proof.** Each source has at most one admissible disposition by the pinned non-equivocation contract. If Paid and Void both held, the very source witnessing Void would also have to choose PREPARE(g), contradicting uniqueness. Both ledgers recognize the same predicate for the buyer's title and seller's economic claim; hence one-sided terminal entitlement is impossible under that rule. Only previously prepared inputs for g get return successors on Void. A foreign choice can *exclude* g without transferring its own rights to g. If both inputs irrevocably prepare different sales, either certificate can exclude the other sale under exact parent binding. An implementation must assign deterministic successor IDs for eligible local returns and guard later spends of them. The trial exercises the NFT successor. Generic payment respend and redistribution are outside its implementation.

A durable, authorized decline consumes that exact parent version too; a continuing owner needs a fresh successor version. Safe permanent closure reports from Phase 2 can substitute only when their quorum and candidate exclusion predicate apply to this decision and every recognized exit. An owner absent before prepare/decline can leave the other's preparation locked indefinitely. A threshold of 5 out of 7 with at most 2 lifetime equivocators ensures an honest intersection by 2q−n>e. Snapshot/key restoration counts against this bound. An honest committee split can stall without Byzantine consensus. Neither silence nor future availability of a quorum automatically resolves votes already locked incompatibly.

**Coordination cost.** Independently owned, freely disposable inputs each need a non-equivocating choice somewhere unless a prior irrevocable constraint already selected it. Two prepares can run in parallel on a common intent; they do not need a later terminal vote for this deterministic conjunction. If discretion remains after both prepare, a new decision or an independently exclusive oracle is necessary. Many NFTs may share one fixed payment outcome only if the entire bundle and its total consideration are bound before preparation. A jointly owned settlement resource could take one later decision, but creating it requires both native assets to admit custody first. Independent allowances permit parallel sales. The bound concerns competing decisions, not signature count, rounds in all consensus protocols or number of independent operators.

**Evidence and liveness.** Vesting, receipt of evidence, collection and onward disposal are distinct. An independent verifier must have each required certificate, prior source admission, policy and agreement. Surviving complete archives permit exercise after executor loss. Replicating a digest alone cannot restore lost votes. Four complete copies survive two withholding holders and one further independent loss; no such multi-host native archive was tested. Failure between certificate creation and archival acknowledgment can permanently stall. Contested eventual decisions require further machinery such as BFT ordering with eventual synchrony, stable membership and live replicas. A lost owner authorization may still block. [State search and counterexamples](results/prepare.txt)

**Limits of the split exclusion rule.** An incompatible certificate must be valid under the *same pinned source policy and version*. A later transaction against a new allowance, a historical sale in another DNA, a differently pinned roster or a mutable reservation that could subsequently be reassigned supplies no exclusion. A void judgment alone is insufficient to claim an unprepared title. The onward verifier additionally demands the exact local title PREPARE for this sale before accepting its return child. For a general wallet implementation, each paid or void successor remains one exclusive right with one stable parent identity even when multiple byte encodings or signer subsets attest the same choice.
