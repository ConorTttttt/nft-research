# Decisions under alternative acceptance contracts

| Candidate | What every recognizing party must enforce | Safety, liveness and cost |
|---|---|---|
| A. Existing native rules | Native balance/credit authorization and exclusion over *all* recognized debits, recovery and migrations. | Lowest new trust if demonstrated. Signed ancestry and collection verified. Irrevocability unresolved. Current native adapter abstains. |
| B. Source-wide certified disposition | Every conflicting operation consumes an exclusive version of the same capacity. Preserve Unyt's arithmetic, fees and entitlement definitions. | Five signatures among seven suffice under the lifetime budget; no guaranteed resolution after a vote split. A sidecar signer cannot constrain unfenced direct spending. |
| C. Certified escrow | Native deposit irrevocably encumbers the right and only escrow-certified exits are recognized. Define creditor, redemption and recovery policy. | Smaller active scope after deposit, potential custody and liquidity cost. Hidden pre-deposit promises or retained native reclaim weaken collateralization. Native enforceability unresolved. |
| D. Unified authority | Both ledgers irrevocably entrust prepared assets to a single authenticated decision. | One terminal order controls both outputs. Joint custody, admission and proof availability are stronger assumptions. Preparing the assets still requires both sources' assent. |
| E. Replicated BFT ordering | A replicated state machine orders incompatible spends and exports, and both domains recognize the results. | With n≥3f+1, authenticated replicas, stable safety state and a reviewed protocol, partial synchrony permits contested progress once a correct leader and quorum communicate. Higher steady-state messaging, log storage, replay and reconfiguration costs. It cannot make an absent owner consent or fence an external exit. [HotStuff](https://doi.org/10.1145/3293611.3331591) |
| F. Conserved allowances plus deterministic bilateral exercise | Reserve mutually independent budgets once. Each prepared input vests under the conjunction of the two irreversible consents. A certified incompatible same-version choice can prove exclusion. | Two independent prepare decisions, zero terminal decision for this restricted contract. Disjoint allowances commute. New bounds on post-prepare discretion and cancellation. Archival recovery remains conditional. |

Select F on top of B or genuinely native equivalent for fixed-price deterministic sales. Use E at the source if contested resolution justifies its cost. C or D make sense only where both economic communities recognize custody and exit rules. If the required change to native recognition is unavailable, keep the two ledgers separate. The improvement lies in removing post-prepare discretion while retaining a conclusive exclusion path, and in defining which exact input can be restored. BFT, escrow budgets, atomic commit and monotone predicates are established prior art. This exercise demonstrates their composition under the specific two-domain acceptance contract. It establishes neither novelty priority nor superiority across all deployment models. [Atomic commit](https://doi.org/10.1145/1132863.1132867)
