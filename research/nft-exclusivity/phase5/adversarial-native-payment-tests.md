# What was rejected, detected, or left unresolved

**Executed official release in an isolated conductor.** Phase 4 reproduced from its bundle commit. A second funded run used the same signed historical record checks and added an independent read of the buyer's prefix from the seller cell. Ten action signatures, sequential indices, hash links, payer identity and DNA genesis were checked through the parked action. The funded buyer acceptance is an ancestor. The five-record verifier additionally binds the acceptance to the historical funding commitment, and the RAVE and seller receipt to the named park. All reads used a disposable, locally configured network. The two agents shared one conductor. No shared testnet or production policy equivalence was tested.

| Scenario | Result and boundary |
|---|---|
| Signed action tampering, altered receipt, DNA substitution, missing or reordered ancestry | REJECTED by independent offline verifiers. Five withheld records were separately treated as unresolved. This rejection occurred in the verifier, not at native authoring time. |
| Reclaim of a separately rejected conventional commitment | EXECUTED_CONDUCTOR. Real request succeeded in the fixture. It did not reclaim the funded sale's parked link. |
| Release ledger arithmetic and recovery eligibility | 46 native public engine tests passed. Fourteen unit-recovery-filter tests passed with the pinned templates and mocked HDK inputs, including rejection of an answered commitment on the supplied chain. These do not replay the private integrity callback or prove complete history. |
| Executor goes away after historical payment, some records lost | A new offline process verified retained evidence. Absence of signatures prevents authenticating the incomplete path. Lab paid/void proof can be recovered from one retained complete object with preprovisioned roots. |
| Actual parked-value refund, delayed collection against native reclaim, overlapping credit and balance spends, two sale agreements | UNRESOLVED at the release economic boundary. Parked-link delete code exists in WASM. No authoritative demonstration that a particular deletion returns spendable capacity or excludes a previously collected allocation. |
| Backdating, stale snapshots, two independently partitioned native conductors, modified coordinator submitting conflicting histories | Native conflict creation and exploit reproduction were not performed. The formal checks vary acceptance and durable-state assumptions. No native prevention or detection verdict assigned. |
| Pending/migrated payments, alternate old-epoch histories, duplicate native NFT import | UNRESOLVED under native acceptance. Fresh disconnected-roster, replay and handoff controls were modeled at the protocol boundary. |
| Warrant or fork standing | Presence of fork/standing types and APIs shows that branches can be represented. No economic rollback, warrant event, or native conflict DETECTED in the recorded experiments. |

The key negative result is about the sufficiency of the evidence, not whether v0.117.0 can actually be induced to sanction conflicting claims. Even a full authentic ancestry of the payer through the park contains no universal statement about other admissible branches or successors. Native prevention would need a complete conflict-coverage argument and a recognition contract for warrants and migration. Safety evaluation of the target excludes operational attack replication. **Observed counterexample to a proposed refund predicate.** In the repeated cooperative sale the buyer and seller each called get_parked_link_standing immediately after parking and again following collection. Both saw Parked with 10 units, then Unparked. Independent signatures and the seller receipt verified in the same run. Thus interpreting Unparked as “seller can never collect” would authorize an NFT refund on a history where the seller already collected. This falsifies that *consumer interpretation* with genuine native records. It is not a demonstration of two native financial dispositions or of a stock Unyt client issuing such a refund. No inference is made about which exact intermediate action changed the standing between the two reads.
