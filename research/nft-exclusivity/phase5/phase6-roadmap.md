# Conditions for funded native acceptance

1. Obtain a pinned release source mapping or authoritative conformance contract for transactor integrity. Resolve the exact effects of parked-link deletion, execution consumption, duplicate collection, capacity overlap, credit adjustments, recovery-agent transitions, migration and fork adjudication. Specify who absorbs a loss when prior claims are revised. The two known Unyt app revisions must be assessed separately.
2. Prototype the smallest **native-recognized** capacity fence with protocol owners. Start with one denomination, prepaid balance and immutable fees. Reserve a conservative allowance at the account before creating portable subrights. Add credit only after proving liability grandfathering or collateralization. Require all exits and redemption paths to honor the fence.
3. Start a new hNFT issuance cohort with a recognized exclusive transfer/export decision from genesis. Bind full source DNA, mint and parent, destination policy and owner. For existing titles, explicitly negotiate which prior receipts and older clients remain economically recognized. Closing an unresolved old title needs a recognized reconciliation authority.
4. Bind a common deterministic joint-prepare predicate in **both** domains and test known-version positives, indeterminate evidence and invalid proofs with independent validators. Exercise source admission, paid and void successor creation on both assets, onward use, rotation and recovery. Admit generalized scripts only with an independently verified effect/guard specification.
5. Require recoverable final delivery before exposing a marketplace's “completed” state. Exercise multi-host archival loss, key rollback protection, committee reconfiguration, cold start and liveness under real network delays. Consider BFT ordering if vote splitting dominates practical reliability. Quantify the choice between blocking and additional coordination.
6. Use signed archives, certified historical checkpoints and authenticated-state's validated-read strategy for portable evidence. DEX reclaim history walks supply comparison cases; they do not prove native money. Threshold signing may shrink certificates but adds interactive rounds and nonce/share continuity. VRFs need pinned, independently administered membership. SSS recovery of an owner secret does not safely restore witness votes. Use time proofs for deadline eligibility only. Keep the published canonical encoding profile independent of this lab's experimental JSON. Agreement UI should expose unavailable, pending, historical collection and final entitlement distinctly. These supporting components were reviewed via the existing pinned [component inventory](../phase3/cross-repository-inventory.md); no new crypto or third-party deployments were necessary.

Accept an irreversible sale only when both native enforcement contracts, complete proof dependencies and downstream recognition are established on the intended network. Until then the lab remains a conformance target and signed real histories remain valuable audit evidence.
