## Reading the counterexamples

One extra rollback-capable identity raises e until the minimum quorum overlap can consist entirely of equivocators. The model then assembles both hidden paid and hidden void. With a separate slot per grant, two distinct buyers' offers can each claim the same funded input even when every honest signer follows the programmed per-grant rule. With an unguarded native reclaim, quorum safety survives inside the overlay and economic conservation fails across the boundary. Removing the grant binding permits use on the wrong NFT. These are conditional histories in a symbolic system. No conflicting native v0.117.0 payment/refund pair was observed.

Independent settlement also fails if the payment can finalize before the title is prepared, if actual seller entitlement depends on a discretionary future collection, or if refund requires only a timeout. A known permanent stall is compatible with every safety check. A hidden decision and a lost last copy of its proof can leave no safe new choice. Phase 2 closure requires authenticated permanent reports satisfying reports minus maximum for any one candidate greater than n−q+e; a refund on the economic domain still needs source-wide coverage. The finite checks do not establish Byzantine consensus, validator completeness, key security, absolute durability or fairness.
