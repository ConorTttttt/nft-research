# Independent verification contract

For native payment, an admissible proof must establish a unique funded source (or uniquely reserved capacity), authorized buyer debit, exact net entitlement to the pinned seller, asset, unit definition, source DNA, agreement and rules, NFT identity and CEL grant, and exclusion across *every* recognized spend, cancellation, reclaim, recovery and epoch change. The issuer and policy must be pinned by the recipient independently. A party supplying both a policy and evidence cannot establish that the policy controls native value. Offline acceptance requires irreversible vesting under that policy. A fresh current balance query is a different claim.

Paid means seller entitlement has irrevocably vested and the conditional grant yields the buyer. Void means the seller can take the predetermined successor and the payment can never vest. Pending covers an unsupported native profile, withheld dependency, missing proof or stalled vote. Invalid covers a demonstrated contradiction, invalid signature, malformed recognized profile or wrong binding. Neither of the last two permits refund. The historical audit operates on local captured JSON. It recomputes the terms anchor, verifies the CEL signatures and grant against explicit pins, and matches the RAVE's allocation and context. It authenticates the title signatures but does not independently authenticate a native action from this JSON. A matching capture stays Pending. The generic Rust native adapter abstains on unclassified data and rejects a recognized lab envelope. [Audit](unyt-integration/audit.py) · [Verifier](prototype/verifier/src/lib.rs)

The separate P4L1 binary profile is a feasibility witness for the proposed source-authority contract. All integers are unsigned big endian. After four ASCII magic bytes come five 32-byte fields (payment realm, source, unit, buyer key, seller key), an eight-byte positive amount, seven ordered title and seven ordered payment public keys. All roster keys must be distinct. The next five 32-byte fields bind NFT source DNA, mint, CEL parent, agreement instance and rule digest. The remaining bytes are a 64-byte seller signature, five increasing (u8 index, 64-byte signature) pairs, a one-byte disposition (0 void, 1 paid), a buyer signature and five ordered payment votes. The verifier accepts exactly 1,559 bytes and verifies at most twelve strict Ed25519 signatures. Unknown versions cannot exercise a successor. A terminal decision requires the buyer for either outcome in this profile. It can stall permanently.

SHA-256 over the literal ASCII domain prefix followed by fixed-size fields yields the policy pin (p4/lab-funding/v1 over policy), grant (p4/grant/v1 over pin and terms), title message (p4/title-reserve/v1 over grant) and terminal message (p4/disposition/v1 over grant and tag). The consumer supplies the pin and intended grant. The externally admitted genesis represents one already funded test unit of account with the specified face amount. Each title witness locks the source title version; payment witnesses lock the funded source across grants. Re-verification is idempotent. A historical successor does not assert fresh ownership after onward transfer. Rosters and rules are immutable in this wire version. Supporting migration requires a different authenticated transition and verification profile. This format does not change Phase 2 or Phase 3 commitments, identify a native Unyt issuer, or confer redemption against native Unyt.

A second native audit profile accepts signed Holochain records. Its Rust helper bounds each input to 1 MiB, checks the expected author/action ID, rehashes the typed action, verifies the signature over the serialized action and, for Create, checks the app entry hash. For CreateLink it returns the authenticated tag. The caller verifies application-specific bindings across the five records. The resulting status is Pending even with all five signatures. These are historical dependencies, without a source-wide exclusion statement.
