# What a native receipt would have to prove

Let a history include issued signatures and locally valid actions, including those hidden from all honest readers. Fix an acceptance contract C specifying the asset, credit/redemption rules, every recognized writer and exit, and whether forks, migration or operator interventions can revise claims. For evidence E let Ext(E,C) be every admissible history consistent with E and every admissible continuation. Define FinalPaid only if in **every** such history the identified seller has the irrevocable agreed net entitlement from authorized, conserved value and no incompatible reclaim is recognized. FinalVoid means that the payment can never finalize and the contract's refund/release is authorized. Neither predicate requires simultaneous delivery of bytes.

The grant binds the source NFT DNA, mint action, current CEL parent, title policy, buyer and seller bindings in each realm, payment DNA, unit definition and fee policy, exact amount, funded source identity and version, agreement instance and version, template and execution-rule hash, terminal rules and recognition policy. A credit limit is capacity. For a credit payment, conservation applies to the remaining credit **and** all outstanding liabilities. Two ordinary debits with different action hashes may compete for the same capacity. Giving each action its own fence after the fact may authorize both.

**Indistinguishability.** If E is possible both when the source is spent exclusively on G and when the same capacity can already back a competing claim, any deterministic verifier returning Paid(E) in the first execution also returns it in the second. A proof system can reject the second history only by exhibiting an exclusion rule or changing admissible histories/acceptance. This is a conditional theorem about the evidence. It does not establish that particular hidden transactions were accepted by Unyt v0.117.0.

**Coverage.** Draw an edge between two decisions whose joint recognition would violate a spending limit or grant exclusivity. Every such edge needs an intersecting durable guard or an immutable rule that makes one side impossible. A terminal witness per sale covers pay-versus-void on that sale. It misses two grants against one source and any unguarded native exit. Reserving against an account balance requires fencing the relevant account capacity at or before partition into independently conserved notes. Deposit-backed notes also require an exclusive permanent lock of their backing and safe redemption. A committee can attest the history it saw without creating those constraints.

**Sufficiency for the restricted ledger.** Assume authentic unique funded genesis, exhaustive recognition of guarded exits, quorums checking the same complete terms and owner consent, and permanent non-equivocation of all but e identities over the validity horizon. Every final disposition of a source has q signatures; two such sets intersect in at least 2q−n. If 2q−n>e, at least one honest member would have to reserve its source for two different digests. This is impossible by construction. Applied also to the title parent, binding the two outcomes makes the agreed seller entitlement and the buyer's vested title equivalent. It does not imply immediate possession, collectability on another ledger, or eventual completion. Five-of-seven tolerates two lifetime equivocations. Even two simultaneously healthy committees cannot provide this argument across an unauthorized reassignment.

**Minimum addition.** The necessary property is conflict coverage, not necessarily another round of seven signatures. A trusted nonforking custodian, native validated consensus over capacity or irrevocably partitioned prepaid rights can implement the same property with different trust and liveness. If native validators/beneficiaries continue to accept direct spends or reclaims outside the guard, an overlay only serializes the overlay currency.
