# Native authority map — Sandbox v0.117.0

The seller's receipt supports credit on an identified history. A capacity bound applies to the liabilities that remain recognized after a transfer, including fees and prior reservations. A parked link or execution ID is created after selection of the funds. It cannot itself establish that its funding was uniquely available. A policy can prepartition a balance into conserved allowances if all recognized native exit paths respect the partition.

| Resource and operation | Author and version/dependencies | Exclusivity question |
|---|---|---|
| Credit issuance/adjustment, ordinary debit | Admitted definitions, applicable limits, payer key and selected ancestry's newest ledger carrier; proposal and commitment reserve different amounts. | Conflicting debits or a reduction below already committed credit. Need grandfathered liabilities or common ordering. |
| Commitment → accept/reject → receipt or reclaim | Counterparty answers a referenced commitment. Reclaim names a rejection and carries the resulting ledger. | Who recognizes answers and recoveries across alternate histories? |
| Parked balance/credit → RAVE → collection | Spender's signed link binds agreement/role, amounts, fee and definition. Executor supplies required inputs. Seller receipt references execution. | Reuse of backing, competing runs, duplicate collection and coexisting exits. The fresh native run observed Unparked after +10 collection. |
| Recovery / refund | System unit recovery template uses specified evidence and a carried recovered set; raises designated recovery authority's credit. | Economic treatment of prior claims across epochs. Missing record, deletion and timeout do not close a liability. |
| Migration / key change / repair | Closing summary + CloseChain, notary attestation and authorized predecessor on successor DNA. | A selected chain's closure must permanently fence old authority and carry *all* recognized liabilities. Restoring a wallet key authenticates its owner; restoring witness state can change lifetime equivocation. |
| Bridge, unit conversion | Cross-network or vault recognition and conversion policy are additional authorities. | Need matching backing and unique redemption on both networks. Operator reversal/risk allocation undocumented here. |
| hNFT transfer/export | Mint DNA + Create action, exact current title version and controller. Existing transfer integrity walks a specified chain top. | Both ordinary transfer and destination-bound export must spend the same exclusivity slot, including during policy changes. |
