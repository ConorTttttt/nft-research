# Contract to be discharged by both native authorities

Fix a recognition policy, the liability horizon, an authenticated input version and its actual spendable amount or title owner. For every admissible set of recognized operations require conservation and compatibility after merging all histories. Coverage must include sets larger than pairs: three debits of four jointly exceed ten although every pair fits. Every such set must either be excluded by disjoint conserved budgets or intersect an enforceable decision domain at or before issuance. For dynamic credit this includes all irrevocably promised outflows. A branch-local version label alone does not serialize histories.

Every recognized ordinary debit, park, terminal allocation, reclaim, migration and recovery must preserve that invariant. Downstream operations may reuse the original choice if they are deterministic, idempotent consequences of it. A separate quorum at every downstream write is unnecessary. Every independent discretionary exit needs an appropriate guard. Unmodified validators accepting an independent exit defeat an overlay. Pin the realm, parent, owner, policy epoch, operation, successor, terms and amount. A new epoch can start only from a certified exclusive old transition. Historic acceptance, availability, final entitlement and current control are distinct.

Sufficiency assumes authentic genesis/admission, exhaustive recognition, unforgeable signatures and permanent non-equivocation of all but e keys. With n=7,q=5,e=2 two quorums intersect in at least three. No two different choices for one parent can both qualify. If an alternative history with incompatible economic recognition is consistent with every verifier input, the same offline verifier must return the same answer in both histories. This proves insufficiency of those inputs conditional on the history model. It does not prove either history is admissible under the private native validator.
